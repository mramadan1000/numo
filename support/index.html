Numo — Support

Need help with Numo, or found a problem? Email hello@mrfilmz.com and we'll get back to you.

  • Your data stays on your iPhone. We can't see it, so please don't send screenshots with private details.
  • Backups: Settings → iCloud & Data → Create a backup file. Keep the password safe; we can't recover it.
  • Language: Settings → Language. Reopen the app to finish the switch.
